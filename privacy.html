Dad Bank Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Dad Bank is a private family app used only by the members of one household.

What the app accesses

When you sign in with Google, the app uses your Google account only to read (and, for the parent, write) the family's Dad Bucks Google Sheets. It does not access your email, contacts, or any other Google data.

What we collect and share

The app has no server and no analytics. It does not collect, store, sell, or share personal data. Access tokens are stored only on your own device.
