Dad Bank Terms of Service

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Dad Bank is a private family app for tracking play-money "Dad Bucks" balances. It is provided to members of one household only, as is, with no warranty.

Dad Bucks have no cash value outside the family's own agreed rules. The family's Google Sheet is the record of balances, and the parent's decisions on balances and rules are final.

Use of Google sign-in is also subject to Google's own terms. See the privacy policy for how data is handled.
